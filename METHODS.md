# Methods and data access

## Study 1

The approved EXP-004 protocol (2026-09-28, DEC-0011) sampled one question from each of 100 unused training documents per corpus, with 30, 40 and 30 documents across 1–2, 3–6 and 7–20 pages. Gold review preceded QA. At each raster level, retrieval used Tesseract and character 1–3-gram TF–IDF. Five arms shared the retrieved page identities within level. In the primary degraded setting, each raster dimension was scaled to 45% before the remaining transformations. Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct used revision `cc594898137f460bfe9f0759e9844b3ce807cfb5`, BF16, SDPA, greedy generation, an 8,000-token text cap and up to 128 new tokens.

The original ANLS scorer lowercases, collapses whitespace and assigns zero to an empty prediction or empty normalized reference set. It thresholds normalized edit distance strictly below 0.5 and takes the best score across full-answer references. On JDocQA, the frozen Japanese function also applies NFKC, removes trailing Japanese full stops and strips one terminal polite copula (です). Units and numerical content are retained. The exact scoring functions are in [scoring.py](scripts/scoring.py). Call `score_value(corpus, prediction, status, answers, formatted=True)` with corpus `jdocqa` or `mpdocvqa` and the frozen references.

The primary contrasts were the PP-OCRv6 image gain and its difference from the Tesseract gain, each on the degraded raster in each corpus. The paired bootstrap used 20,000 stratified resamples and 98.75% marginal percentile intervals. The stored configuration preserves the seed, corpus order and quantiles. [Configuration](study1_sampling.json)

## Study 2

EXP-005 Main600 was approved on 2026-10-23 (DEC-0028), with the pre-inference capacity amendment DEC-0031. The main roster was fixed before outcomes. Previously used pilot documents remained separate. Fresh PP-OCRv6 medium read the selected degraded pages. Using clearer source pages, an editor corrected the transcript before seeing the question. One reader per document answered from the retained edited text and supplied evidence. Eligibility required a resolved source, a response satisfying the frozen answer components and support in the retained text. Screening yielded 372 eligible documents out of 600. On these documents Qwen ran five arms and InternVL3.5-8B-HF ran the two edited-text arms, revision `741a7d03020411e666c6109218ab71e08151ef86`.

Within each question, identical answers shared a first-pass grade from rater A. The primary analysis used these records under a decision made before linking the grades to model or condition. The four unresolved grades were allowed their possible binary values with those links preserved. The primary contrast was the Qwen image gain on observed OCR minus the gain on edited text, standardized with weights 0.30, 0.40 and 0.30 across eligible length strata. The paired bootstrap used 20,000 resamples, PCG64 seed 20261026 and 97.5% marginal envelopes. The conservative bounds used four integer thresholds in each of three strata for each of two corpora, allocating 0.05/24 to each two-sided binomial component. Both procedures had to exclude zero on the same side. Table II reports standardized arm accuracy as a percentage and paired gains in percentage points. Each contrast retains the shared label assignments across arms. Table III reports unweighted counts.

[intervals.py](scripts/intervals.py) contains the archived Study 1 bootstrap and Study 2 paired-bootstrap, Clopper–Pearson and direction routines. This optional module also imports SciPy (locally checked with version 1.18.1). Study 1 needs the paired rows and [sampling configuration](study1_sampling.json). Study 2 needs the within-document lower and upper outcomes and indices grouped by stratum. Aggregate CSVs suffice to reproduce the *display* of the intervals.

## Controlled records

Rebuilding the paired estimates and intervals requires one row per document, its length stratum, predictions linked across input conditions, and the first-pass grades shared within each question. Recovering the screen requires the retained text, reader responses, evidence and eligibility decisions. The controlled archive also holds the protocols, pre-inference amendment, raster and inference configurations, and frozen evaluation records. B's item-level verification and grading records are unavailable. A new round of human grading would produce a different set of observations.
